# Reference batch 3 — generic analysis (style + archetypes only)

Source: 144 frames (images 250–393) from a third-party Thai narration channel, a Japanese-setting urban-legend story
(school toilet ghost, slit-mouth woman, delivery rider, protective charm). Used ONLY to learn style, archetypes,
location types and insert/overlay types. Never crop, trace or copy their pixels, characters 1:1, names, logos, text.

## Style notes (same family as before)
- Flat vector cutout puppets, big rounded head, dot eyes with highlight, blush, wavy "nervous" mouth, side-profile
  standing pose is the default. Soft film grain, vignette, heavy background blur on close-ups, white fade transitions.
- Backgrounds: clean flat geometry with soft gradients, light shafts (streetlight cones, window light), dust specks.
- Text baked into their scenes is Japanese (signs, menus, vending labels, toilet sign, door indicator,
  charm label, delivery box). For OUR assets every such text must be ENGLISH (or blank).

## Character archetypes (generic — draw ORIGINAL versions)
- Young male delivery rider: spiky black hair, yellow rain jacket with grey hood, black pants, white sneakers,
  teal insulated delivery backpack (label in English, e.g. "DELIVERY"), small red charm on the strap.
- Young male security guard: navy cap with gold badge, navy uniform jacket, shoulder emblem (textless shield),
  grey trousers, earring, mole.
- Schoolgirl with yellow safety hat: low ponytail, navy blazer, grey pleated skirt, white socks, brown shoes.
- Ghost schoolgirl (bob + bangs): pale skin, white blouse, red suspender skirt; variant with unnaturally wide
  stitched grin (horror expression "wide_grin").
- Masked ghost woman: very long straight black hair, white surgical mask, beige trench coat, red shoes,
  white gloves; unmasked close-up with long stitched smile scar (expression "slit_smile").
- Old male caretaker/janitor: grey short hair, khaki work jacket, white towel around neck, key ring.
- Old woman landlady: white hair bun with hairpin, round glasses, purple cardigan over beige top, dark skirt.
- Restaurant dishwasher (reuse rider in white tee + navy apron).
Expressions seen: nervous wavy mouth, wide-eyed shock (white eyes small pupils), crying with tears, eyes-shut
cringe (><), relieved smile, smirk. Poses: walk, bend over exhausted (hands on knees), ride bicycle (side view),
pull suitcase, knock on door (hand POV), hug from behind, kneel.

  windows with tape X, dark curtains. Side variant with lockers and two desks.

  "SHRINE"), houses with lit windows, dusk purple sky. Day variant.
- Long night alley/walkway: one-point perspective, concrete walls, a row of lamp posts.
- Two-storey wooden apartment (exterior): external steel staircase, doors 101/102/201/202, mailboxes, bicycles.
- Tatami room interior: sliding door, wall calendar (number only), paper lantern, low table, window.
- Small ramen-shop kitchen: white tiles, red-rim plate stacks, menu tags (English: RAMEN ¥800 / MISO ¥900 /
