# Reference batch 4 — generic analysis (style + archetypes only)

Source: 99 frames (images 394–492) from a third-party Thai narration channel, a Thai urban sci-fi thriller
(phone-repair mall, "death-date prediction" app, night market, laundromat, data center). Used ONLY to learn style,
archetypes, location types and insert/overlay types. Never crop, trace or copy their pixels, characters 1:1,
names, logos or text. All plot-specific names (app name, company name, people names, places) are dropped.

## Style notes (same family as before)
- Flat vector cutout puppets, big round head, dot eyes, blush, sweat drop, wavy nervous mouth; anger-vein mark;
  closed-eye laugh (^ ^); bruised/injured face variant (black eye, scratches, torn sleeve).
- Bright clean mall interiors (soft white fog/bloom, neon shop signs), dark navy night streets, rain streaks,
  bokeh-blur close-up backgrounds, desaturated flashback (greyscale) look.
- Baked text in their scenes is Thai. For OUR assets every such text must be ENGLISH (or blank / numbers only).

## Character archetypes (draw ORIGINAL versions)
- Young male phone-repair technician: curly black hair, square black glasses with taped temple, rolled-sleeve
  denim shirt over grey tee, black jeans, grey sneakers, waist tool pouch with screwdrivers, optional head loupe.
  Variant: injured (bruises, torn sleeve, cracked lens).
- Middle-aged flashy shop owner: blonde slicked quiff with shaved sides, blue pineapple-print short-sleeve shirt,
  thick gold chain, khaki cargo shorts, black flip-flops; sunglasses variant.
- Old male security guard: bald top / white hair, big white moustache, navy uniform, lanyard ID card, sweat drop.
- Police officer (generic): khaki-brown uniform & cap, belt; textless badge.
- Young woman hacker: long black low ponytail, side bangs, black jacket over green hoodie, grey backpack, smirk.
- Disguise woman: wide straw sun hat, long brown hair, glasses, denim vest, maroon long skirt, wristwatch.
- Old woman noodle vendor: grey curly hair in net, yellow blouse, patterned apron.
- Elderly building cleaner: light-blue work shirt, light-blue cap, grey trousers (pushes trolley / mop cart).
- Delivery rider: orange jacket, white helmet, orange delivery box backpack (label "DELIVERY" or blank).
- Old motorbike driver: grey jacket, black cap.
Expressions seen: laugh (closed eyes), angry with vein, scared wide-eyed, sweaty nervous, smirk, determined.
Poses: sit on stool, run pulling another by the hand, climb fence, lie on floor, hold phone, offer hand.
